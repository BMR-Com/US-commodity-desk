How ideas are built (from the research summaries): trend = sign of 12-month return confirmed by the 6-month return and the 200-day average (time-series momentum). Carry = front contract vs the same month next year, annualised; ignored for Nat Gas, Live Cattle and Lean Hogs because their curves are seasonal. Trend + carry agree → full size; trend alone → half; conflict → flat. Size scales with 1/volatility (10% vol target per full position) and is halved when S&P 500 < 200-day and VIX > 25. COT overlay halves trend trades into crowded positioning and turns contrarian COT setups into small tactical ideas only once price confirms. Stops 2×ATR(14), targets 2R. Prices from Yahoo Finance: trend measures use the continuous front-month series with roll gaps removed (grains, softs, livestock, Nat Gas); reference levels, RSI, ATR and 1w/4w moves use the actual tradable contract shown next to the price. COT index = where funds' net position sits between its low (0) and high (100) over the look-back. Flow σ = this week's net change vs the typical weekly change over 3 years. Driver splits the change into new buying / short covering / long liquidation / fresh shorting. P/M %ile = producer/merchant (commercial) net percentile — commercials at the opposite extreme to funds is a classic divergence. Setup (−100…+100) is contrarian: positive = crowded short with commercials long and flow starting to turn up; it is penalised while flow is still running hard in the crowded direction. Flags: 🔥 squeeze fuel · ⚠️ liquidation risk · ↕️ spec/commercial divergence · ⏩ fast repositioning.
