USDA Reschedules Reports Affected by Lapse in Federal Funding

Issued Feb. 6, 2019 by the Agricultural Statistics Board of the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, National Agricultural Statistics Service. For
more information contact Sue King, sue.king@nass.usda.gov, 202-690-8122.
