USDA Reschedules Reports Affected by Lapse in Federal Funding


Issued Jan. 30, 2019 by the Agricultural Statistics Board of the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, National Agricultural Statistics Service. For
more information contact Sue King, sue.king@nass.usda.gov, 202-690-8122.

The USDA’s National Agricultural Statistics Service’s (NASS)
Agricultural Statistics Board has begun rescheduling reports that were
affected by the lapse in federal funding. During the lapse, NASS was not
able to collect data nor issue reports. The following reports have been
rescheduled and others will be announced as soon as they are determined:

* Broiler Hatchery (Jan. 30) is delayed
* Egg Products (Jan. 30) is delayed
