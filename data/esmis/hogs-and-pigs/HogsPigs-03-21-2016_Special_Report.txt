Reminder: NASS to Release Reports at 12 Noon on Friday, March 25 

Issued March 17, 2015 by the Agricultural Statistics Board of the U.S.
Department of Agriculture’s National Agricultural Statistics Service.
For more information contact Sue King, sue.king@nass.usda.gov,
202-690-8122. 

The National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS) is issuing this
reminder that it will release reports on Friday, March 25 at 12:00 noon
ET, rather than at 3:00 p.m. ET. The noon release time is normal
practice on a Friday before a holiday weekend and is published in
advance on the official calendar. The reports to be released on Friday
are the Quarterly Hogs and Pigs report and the weekly Peanut Prices
report. 

All NASS reports and the publication calendar are available at
www.nass.usda.gov.
