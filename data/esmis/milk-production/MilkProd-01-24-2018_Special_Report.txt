USDA Reschedules Reports Delayed by Lapse of Federal Funding 

The National Agricultural Statistics Service's Agricultural Statistics
Board has rescheduled reports that were affected by the lapse in federal
funding. The January 22 Chicken and Eggs (3:00 p.m. ET) and Cotton
Ginnings (Noon ET) reports will be released today, January 23, at the
regularly scheduled times. The January 23 Cold Storage (3:00 p.m. ET)
and Milk Production (3:00 p.m. ET) reports are rescheduled for release
tomorrow, January 24, at the regularly scheduled time. All other reports
will remain unchanged on the ASB calendar. The reports will be available
at www.nass.usda.gov/Publications.
