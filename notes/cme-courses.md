# CME Institute courses — notes for the primers

Source: CME Group education courses (cmegroup.com/education/courses), read 2026-10-19. Summaries in our own words; the course videos/text are CME's.

## Introduction to Dairy (13 lessons) — read

**Contracts.** Six products, all cash-settled on Globex, 24 consecutive months listed: Class III and Class IV milk (200,000 lb), NDM and dry whey (44,000 lb), butter and cheese (20,000 lb). Class III = "cheese milk" (cheese plus its by-product whey); Class IV = butter/NDM milk.

**Settlement chain.** Plants selling ≥1 million lb/yr of cheese, butter, NDM or dry whey report sales weekly to USDA AMS (mandatory since 2010; intra-company, resale and subsidised export sales excluded). AMS publishes the weekly National Dairy Products Sales Report (NDPSR) → monthly Announcement of Class and Component Prices → that first-published print settles the futures (later revisions ignored). Last trading day is the business day before the AMS release; the release is on or before the 5th, so a month's settlement can land late in the month itself or early the next, and the number of NDPSR weeks in a month varies (4 or 5).

**CME spot dairy (CME Direct auction).** Physically delivered, commercial inventory tool: block and barrel cheese, butter, dry whey, NDM, in short daily sessions late morning CT. Spot prices feed plant sales prices, hence NDPSR, hence class prices — the spot call is the leading indicator for where the month's class price settles.

**FMMOs.** Minimum farm milk prices by class (I fluid, II soft products, III hard cheese, IV butter/powder), set by formula from NDPSR product prices. Amended by hearing plus producer referendum.

**S&D drivers (course framing).** Milk produced every day, spring flush peak, summer heat stress dip; milk is perishable so surplus goes into storable products (cheese, butter, NDM, whey) which buffer supply. Demand: domestic retail/food service dominant; exports growing, led by NDM and whey (Mexico, Canada, China, SE Asia, MENA), cheese/butter exported mainly when the world is short.

**Basis.** Producer basis = mailbox price minus Class III futures (Class III used as proxy even though producers are paid a blend of classes). Component standards on the milk check: 3.5% fat, 3.1% protein, 5.9% other solids.

**Spreads/strips.** Strips up to 18 consecutive months at an average price; calendar spreads; Class III–Class IV; the "Class III crush" (long Class III vs short cheese, whey, butter); exchange spread margin credits.

**Where the course is out of date — check against AMS before using in the primer:**
- Market statistics are 2016–2017 vintage.
- It lists 10 FMMOs; California became an order in Nov 2024 (11).
- Formulas predate the June 2025 FMMO amendments (make allowances, component factors, Class I mover, cheese price used in the protein formula). The Class III/IV rebuild must use the current AMS formulas.
- Spot session times and products should be re-checked on the CME spot dairy page.
