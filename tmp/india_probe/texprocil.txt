            <div class="col-md-12 col-xs-12 about-home">
            	 
The Cotton Textiles Export Promotion Council is the first Council set up after Independence by the Government of India in 1954 is an autonomous, nonprofit body dedicated to promotion of exports. The Cotton Textiles Export Promotion Council, popularly known as TEXPROCIL has been the international face of cotton textiles from India facilitating exports worldwide.&nbsp; Texprocil has a membership of around 2,000 companies spread across major textile clusters in India.&nbsp; Its members are well established manufacturers and exporters of cotton textile products like Cotton, Yarns, Fabrics and Home Textiles, showcasing a dazzling array of products across the value chain. The Council connects international buyers with appropriate suppliers and facilitates interaction that enables them to source their specific needs.&nbsp; It also provides information on India's competitive advantages, its export environment and updated position in the global market place. Texprocil provides regular updates on international product trends, trade related issues, advances in technology and the latest developments in the industry, as well as existing and emerging markets.&nbsp; It also undertakes regular market research, organises participation in international trade fairs, holds its own Buyer Seller Meets and facilitates international trade missions in  ...                    <br />
