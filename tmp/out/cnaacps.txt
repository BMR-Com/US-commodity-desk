Spot cotton quotations for color 41, leaf 4, staple 34, mike 35-36 and 43-49, strength readings of 27.0-28.9 grams per tex, uniformity
of 81.0-81.9 units in the designated spot markets averaged 65.35 cents per pound for the 2025-2026 marketing year, up from 63.83
cents for the 2024-2025 marketing year. The marketing year's highest daily quotation for the base quality occurred on May 11, 2026,
at 82.98 cents per pound and the lowest quotation was 57.70 cents on February 6, 2026. The lowest monthly average for the
marketing year was 60.33 cents per pound in February 2026 and the highest was 77.15 cents per pound in May 2026.

Quotations for color 31, leaf 3, staple 36, mike 35-36 and 43-49, strength readings of 27.0-28.9 grams per tex, uniformity of 81.0-
81.9 units in the designated spot markets averaged 71.72 cents per pound for the 2025-2026 market year, down from 70.22 cents
for the 2024-2025 market year.

The average price received by farmers for Upland cotton in July was 64.60 cents per pound in the 2025-2026 marketing year. The
2024-2025 marketing year average price was 63.20 cents, compared to the 2023-2024 marketing year of 76.10 cents, according to
the National Agricultural Statistics Service, USDA. The marketing year average price is monthly prices weighted by monthly
marketings during the period August through the following July, with no allowances for unredeemed loans.

Spot cotton transactions for Upland and American Pima in the designated markets totaled 1,537,240 running bales in the 2025-2026
marketing year, up from 1,010,518 bales in the 2024-2025 marketing year and from 842,378 bales in 2023-2024.
