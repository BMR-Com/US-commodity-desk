                                                  American Pima (AP)
Spot cotton trading was inactive. Supplies were moderate. Demand was light. No forward contracting or domestic mill
activity was reported. Average local spot prices were steady. Mill inquiries were light.
   In Arizona, severe weather including flash flooding, tornadoes, and hail activity was reported. Daytime highs ranged
from the 60s to the 90s across the Far Western region, while nighttime lows cooled into the 50s and 70s. In central
Arizona, damage was reported to power lines and homes in some of the cotton-growing areas of the state, including
Overgaard, AZ. As a result, damage assessments will be made to determine what damage, if any, was done to the crop.
In other parts of the region, the crop advanced steadily, and some areas expanded picker harvesting. Ginning continued
in Yuma, AZ. In the SJV, harvest was expanding in Tulare and Kern Counties in California. Gins increased operations
to accommodate accumulating modules on gin yards.
